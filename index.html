About Us

Higher Up Consulting

is a founder-led, veteran-owned restoration and roofing firm built on discipline, precision, and elevated standards. With over four years of hands-on industry experience, we specialize in restoring residential properties using higher-grade materials and proven systems designed for long-term performance.

We don’t believe in shortcuts or bargain materials. Every project is executed with attention to detail, modern code compliance, and the same level of care we would expect for our own homes.

At Higher Up Consulting, quality is intentional—and trust is earned.

Our Services

Higher Up Consulting provides tailored roofing and exterior restoration solutions, offering both retail services and insurance claim guidance. Our primary focus is residential properties, with the capability to support commercial and interior restoration needs.

We work closely with homeowners and property owners to align scope, materials, and execution with modern standards and exceed them, utilizing higher-grade roofing systems and components engineered for durability, performance, and long-term protection. Clear communication and thoughtful planning remain central to every project.

From initial inspection through completion, each project is managed with discipline, transparency, and refined attention to detail, delivering confidence, consistency, and peace of mind.
